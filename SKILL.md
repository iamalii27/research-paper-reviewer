\---

name: research-paper-reviewer

description: Reviews academic research papers or sections for quality and clarity. Use when reviewing a research paper, checking academic writing, evaluating an abstract, introduction, methodology, results, discussion, or conclusion section, or when asking for feedback on scholarly writing.

allowed-tools: Read

\---



When reviewing a research paper or section:



1\. Read through the entire content carefully before giving any feedback.



2\. Output the review in the following structured format:



\## Paper/Section Identified

One sentence stating what section or paper you are reviewing.



\## Strengths

\- List up to 5 strong points (clear argument, good structure, strong evidence, etc.)



\## Weaknesses

\- List up to 5 areas that need improvement (vague claims, missing citations, weak transitions, etc.)



\## Academic Tone Assessment

Rate the academic tone as: Strong / Acceptable / Needs Improvement

Briefly explain why in 2-3 sentences.



\## Logical Flow Assessment

Rate the logical flow as: Strong / Acceptable / Needs Improvement

Briefly explain why in 2-3 sentences.



\## Specific Suggestions

\- Give 3 to 5 concrete, actionable suggestions the writer can apply immediately.



\## Overall Score

Rate the writing out of 10 and justify it in one sentence.
